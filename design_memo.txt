Design Memo

A binary tree is a good pick for the doctor structure because it shows who reports to who. Every doctor can have a left and right report, so it is easy to see how the hospital team is organized. The top doctor is the root, and the doctors below them are connected as child nodes. This makes the structure easier to follow than one long list of names. The tree in this project is not sorted alphabetically. Instead, doctors are placed under a specific manager.
Tree traversals can be useful in different situations. Preorder looks at the manager first and then the doctors below them. This could help when sending instructions from the top down. Inorder visits the left side, then the manager, then the right side. It gives another way to display the structure, although it does not automatically put names in alphabetical order. Postorder visits the doctors below a manager before the manager. This could be useful when collecting reports from staff before the manager reviews them.
A min heap is useful for the emergency room because patients should not always be treated in the order they arrive. A patient with a lower urgency number needs attention sooner. The heap keeps the most urgent patient at the top. When a new patient arrives, the heap moves them into the correct position. When a patient is removed, the heap fixes its order again. This helps the system handle changes quickly without sorting the whole list every time. In a real hospital, doctors would still make the actual medical decisions.
